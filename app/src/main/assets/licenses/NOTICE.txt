FFmpeg audio decoding

This application includes FFmpeg 6.1.4, licensed under LGPL 2.1 or later,
and the AndroidX Media3 1.8.0 FFmpeg extension, licensed under Apache 2.0.
The application source remains MIT licensed. FFmpeg was built without
GPL or nonfree components. There is no warranty for these libraries.
The Media3 JNI integration is modified to initialize and retain TrueHD channel
layouts, split grouped access units, log FFmpeg errors, and preserve decoded
PCM when its output buffer grows. The corresponding
patch is included in ffmpeg-decoder-sources.zip and the application repository.
